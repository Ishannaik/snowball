# Compile instructions for the research wiki

- Sources are dated research reports built from forum threads. Keep the date and the forum
  (Reddit, Hacker News, GitHub, Discourse) next to each claim.
- Prefer updating an existing concept or entity page over creating a near-duplicate. Reuse the
  existing page names in the index when a concept already exists.
- Extract at most 12 concepts per source. Pick the ones a reader would look up later: tools,
  programs, techniques, products, conditions. Skip generic words.
- Keep concrete numbers, versions, settings and deadlines. Mark "unconfirmed" claims as such.
- Health topics: write "anecdotal forum reports, not medical advice" on the page.
- Short plain sentences. No em-dashes.
